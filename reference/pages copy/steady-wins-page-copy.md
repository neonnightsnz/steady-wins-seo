\# Steady Wins — Page Copy



\### All 9 listings, written to the Brand Voice Guidelines v2



A few adjustments made across every page to stay inside the Guardrails section of the voice doc — flagging them once here instead of repeating on each page:



\- \*\*No unqualified "100% guarantee."\*\* Each guarantee below is qualified — it says what specifically happens if the client isn't happy, matching what the original docs actually promised elsewhere (e.g., the PBN Whip's "4 week movement" clause).

\- \*\*Monthly vs. one-time is stated in the subhead\*\*, not buried in the price line, on every recurring service.

\- \*\*"Unlimited"/"never-ending" traffic claims got a mechanism attached\*\* (keyword count, link volume, cadence) so the headline can still swing big without contradicting the deliverables.

\- \*\*Scarcity lines are capacity-based\*\*, not countdown-timer fake. Swap in your real cap if the number below isn't accurate — the line only works if it's true.
