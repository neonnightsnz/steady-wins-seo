\## 02 / How we work



\*\*Nine ways we build your engine.\*\*



We don't run one tactic and call it a strategy. Every engagement pulls from the same nine disciplines — applied in whatever order actually moves your business forward first.



1\. \*\*On-page optimization\*\* — structuring and refining your content so search engines can read it, and rank it, correctly.

2\. \*\*Keyword research \& targeting\*\* — identifying the terms your real buyers search, and building them into your content and metadata properly.

3\. \*\*Technical SEO\*\* — making sure your site is fast, mobile-friendly, and fully crawlable — the foundation everything else stands on.

4\. \*\*Link building\*\* — earning links from other sites that build your authority instead of putting it at risk.

5\. \*\*Content marketing\*\* — creating content worth ranking, built around what your audience actually wants to know.

6\. \*\*Social media marketing\*\* — extending your content's reach and keeping your brand visible where your audience already is.

7\. \*\*Local SEO\*\* — making sure the businesses searching nearby can actually find you, through citations, reviews, and local optimization.

8\. \*\*Conversion rate optimization\*\* — because traffic that doesn't convert isn't a result, it's a vanity metric.

9\. \*\*Analysis \& tracking\*\* — measuring what's actually working, and adjusting the plan based on data instead of guesswork.
