Before commissioning another page, write down what it needs to do. Use these questions as a working checklist.

## Who is reading?

Choose one audience for the page. Describe what that person already knows and the question they need answered.

## What should the page explain?

List the information a reader needs to understand your offer. Start with specific details you can support, rather than broad claims about being the best.

## What comes next?

Choose a useful next action. A reader might compare packages, read a related explanation, or prepare an enquiry. Give that action a clear label and a working destination.

## Keep the brief close

Use the answers as a review checklist when the page is drafted. Cut passages that do not serve the reader or the page's purpose.
