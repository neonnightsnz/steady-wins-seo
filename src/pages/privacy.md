## Your growth brief

The growth brief builder processes the information you enter in your browser. It creates a text file when you select **Download my brief**. The form does not send the information to Steady Wins or save it in this website's storage.

You control where you save or share the downloaded file. Do not include confidential information in your brief.

## Display preferences

The website remembers your colour-theme preference in your browser's local storage. You can remove that preference by clearing this website's browser data.

## Website delivery

Your browser requests pages and assets from the website host. Hosting services may process technical request information to deliver the site. This page will be updated with the operator's contact details and applicable hosting information before public launch.
